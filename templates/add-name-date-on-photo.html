Frequently Asked Questions

Is printing the Date of Photo (DOP) compulsory for SSC and UP Police recruitment? ▼

Yes. Most official SSC notifications (CGL, CHSL, MTS, GD Constable) and State Police recruitment rules state that the photograph must not be older than 3 months from the notice date and must clearly display the candidate's name and the date the photo was taken printed at the bottom.

Will the center guide lines be visible on my downloaded photograph? ▼

No. The dashed center lines are visual positioning guides on your screen only. When you click download, only your clean photograph and the official name/date strip are exported.

What are the exact photo dimensions generated by this tool? ▼

The output image is formatted to standard Indian government exam proportions (826 × 1062 pixels @ 300 DPI), perfectly matching the official 3.5 cm × 4.5 cm passport standard in razor-sharp clarity.

Are my personal photographs or name details stored on your servers? ▼

Never. SmartPicTools is 100% client-side. The image canvas rendering runs entirely inside your browser's temporary memory, and all data is permanently erased the moment you close the tab.
