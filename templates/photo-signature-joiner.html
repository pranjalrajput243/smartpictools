Frequently Asked Questions

What is the official photo and signature size for SSC & UP Police forms? ▼

Most portals require an image width of 350px and height of 500px, where the photo occupies the top 75% and signature occupies the bottom 25%.

How does the Clean Paper Shadow algorithm eliminate noise? ▼

It calculates the local background paper luminance using morphological subtraction. Shadows and beige paper are neutralized into 100% spotless white, keeping ink strokes sharp and dark without any noisy dots.
