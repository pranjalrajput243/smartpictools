Frequently Asked Questions

What is the official photo and signature size for SSC & UP Police forms? ▼

Most portals require an image width of 350px and height of 500px, where the photo occupies the top 75% and signature occupies the bottom 25%, with total file size under 50KB.

Does the tool clean yellow shadows from mobile camera signatures? ▼

Yes! Our 'Whiten Signature Paper' feature automatically removes paper shadows and yellowish tints, converting it into a clean studio-scanned signature.

Are my photos uploaded to any server? ▼

No. Everything runs 100% locally in your device's browser using HTML5 Canvas. Your photos never leave your device.
