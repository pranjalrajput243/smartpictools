Frequently Asked Questions

What is the official photo and signature size for SSC & UP Police forms? ▼

Most portals require an image width of 350px and height of 500px, where the photo occupies the top 75% and signature occupies the bottom 25%.

How does the Whiten Signature Paper feature work? ▼

It smoothly stretches lighting levels, turning yellow paper and room shadows into pure clean white, while darkening the pen ink so it looks professionally scanned.

Are my photos uploaded to any server? ▼

No. Everything runs 100% locally in your device's browser using HTML5 Canvas. Your photos never leave your device.
