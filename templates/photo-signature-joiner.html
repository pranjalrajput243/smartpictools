Frequently Asked Questions

What is the official photo and signature size for SSC & UP Police forms? ▼

Most portals require an image width of 350px and height of 500px, where the photo occupies the top 75% and signature occupies the bottom 25%.

How does the Whiten Signature Paper feature work? ▼

It uses adaptive local background division, turning yellow paper, phone shadows, and vignette into pure clean white, while keeping pen ink strokes crisp and dark.
