Frequently Asked Questions

What does Lock Proportions mean? ▼

It locks the ratio between width and height. When you change width, height adjusts automatically so your image never looks stretched or squished.

Can I set custom dimensions for online exam requirements (like 200×230 px)? ▼

Yes! Simply uncheck 'Lock Proportions' and enter the exact required pixels (e.g. 200 width and 230 height) to meet official portal specifications.
