Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between resizing dimensions and compressing an image? ▼

Resizing changes the physical pixel count (Width and Height in pixels, e.g. from 4000×3000 px to 800×600 px). Compressing reduces the file weight (size in KB or MB) without necessarily changing the resolution. Both help optimize images for web speed and portal compliance.

How do I resize an image to exact specifications for government job portals (e.g. 200 × 230 px)? ▼

Simply uncheck the 'Lock Proportions' checkbox. Enter 200 in the Width box and 230 in the Height box. The tool will format the output to exact pixel parameters without being constrained by the original camera ratio.

Does resizing an image cause blurriness or loss of quality? ▼

Downscaling (reducing dimensions from large to smaller pixels) keeps the image sharp and increases rendering speed. Upscaling (stretching a small picture to very large pixels) can cause softening, which is why keeping 'Lock Proportions' enabled is recommended for clean scaling.

Can I resize heavy iPhone HEIC photos directly? ▼

Yes. Modern Apple iPhone photos (.heic/.heif) are decoded client-side inside your browser, allowing you to set exact pixel dimensions and download a standard, lightweight JPEG file instantly.

Are my photos uploaded to any remote server or stored in a database? ▼

Never. SmartPicTools is built on a 100% private, client-side HTML5 Canvas architecture. Your pictures remain inside your local browser memory and are permanently cleared when you close the tab.
