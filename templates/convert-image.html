Frequently Asked Questions

What is the benefit of converting to SVG? ▼

SVG (Scalable Vector Graphics) is an XML-based format that scales smoothly to any screen resolution without losing clarity. It is widely used by UI designers and modern websites.

When should I use TIFF format? ▼

TIFF preserves maximum uncompressed image detail without loss, making it the industry standard for professional photography, publishing, and commercial printing.
