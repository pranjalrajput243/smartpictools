Frequently Asked Questions

Which format is best for fast website loading speed: AVIF, WEBP, or JPG? ▼

AVIF and WEBP are next-generation image formats recommended by Google. AVIF provides up to 50% smaller file sizes than JPG at equivalent visual quality, dramatically improving web page speed and Core Web Vitals. WEBP offers broad 98%+ browser compatibility. Converting your web pictures to WEBP or AVIF is the fastest way to accelerate website load times.

What happens to transparent backgrounds when converting a PNG to JPG? ▼

The JPEG specification does not support alpha transparency. When you convert a transparent PNG, our tool automatically fills the transparent areas with a clean, solid white background, preventing black background glitches commonly produced by basic converters.

Can I convert Apple iPhone HEIC photos to JPG or PNG on Windows and Android? ▼

Yes. Photos taken on modern iPhones (.heic/.heif) are decoded client-side inside your browser, allowing you to convert them directly into universal JPG, PNG, or PDF formats without needing iTunes, Apple software, or third-party desktop codecs.

Never. SmartPicTools operates on a strict zero-upload architecture. Everything is converted locally in your browser memory and all data buffers are permanently erased the moment you close your browser tab.
