Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

At SmartPicTools, privacy is not just a policy—it is our core engineering principle. This document outlines the types of information we collect and how we safeguard user data.

1. Image Data Security & In-Memory Execution

When you upload an image to our platform for compression, resizing, conversion, or watermarking, the image is processed dynamically in temporary server RAM. We never store, inspect, replicate, or index your images. As soon as your download completes, all memory buffers are wiped permanently.

2. Google AdSense & Third-Party Cookies

We may partner with third-party advertising vendors, including Google AdSense. Google uses cookies (including the DoubleClick cookie) to serve ads to users based on their visits to this site and other sites across the Internet. You can opt out of personalized advertising at any time by visiting Google Ads Settings.

3. Log Files

Like standard websites, SmartPicTools utilizes anonymized log files. Information inside log files includes Internet Protocol (IP) addresses, browser types, Internet Service Providers (ISP), date/time stamps, and referring pages. These logs are non-personally identifiable and used solely for monitoring server uptime and security.

4. Contact Information

If you have any questions or data inquiries regarding our privacy standards, contact us at: support@smartpictools.com.
