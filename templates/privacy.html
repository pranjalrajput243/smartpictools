Privacy Policy

Your photos never leave your device. 100% In-Browser Privacy.

1. Zero File Retention Policy

At SmartPicTools (accessible from https://smartpictools.in), user privacy is our foundational promise. We do not store, view, catalog, or share any of the images you process. All image transformations (compress, crop, resize, watermark, rotate, convert) execute entirely client-side inside your browser's local RAM and are permanently wiped immediately upon download.

2. Information We Collect

We do not require user account registration, passwords, or personal billing information. The only information collected is standard anonymous technical telemetry (such as browser type and screen resolution) to optimize tool speed. If you submit suggestions via our feedback box, your optional email address is used solely to reply to your inquiry.

3. Cookies & Advertising Partners

SmartPicTools may use standard cookies to ensure website settings load seamlessly. Third-party partners, including Google, may use cookies to serve relevant contextual advertisements. You may disable cookies at any time directly through your individual web browser preferences.

4. GDPR & CCPA Compliance

Because we do not store, harvest, or monetize user photo files or personal identifiers, SmartPicTools complies fully with European GDPR and California CCPA privacy standards. You possess the right to use all our utilities with complete anonymity.

5. Privacy Contact

For any questions regarding our security and privacy standards, contact us anytime at support@smartpictools.in.
