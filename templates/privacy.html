Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026. Your images never leave your computer or phone.

1. Zero File Retention Policy

At SmartPicTools (accessible from https://smartpictools.in), user privacy is our highest priority. We do not store, view, catalog, or share any of the images you process. All image transformations (compress, crop, resize, watermark, rotate, convert) occur client-side inside your browser's execution memory and are instantly cleared once the download completes or the tab closes.

2. Information We Collect

We do not require user account registration, login credentials, or credit card information. The only information collected is anonymous technical telemetry (such as browser type, referring pages, and device operating system) to optimize speed and maintain platform uptime. If you choose to submit feedback via our suggestion box, your optional email address is stored solely to provide customer assistance.

3. Cookies & Web Beacons

Like any other professional website, SmartPicTools uses standard cookies to store visitor preferences and optimize webpage delivery. Third-party partners, including Google AdSense, may place cookies on your browser to serve contextual advertisements based on previous web visits. You have the right to disable cookies at any time through your individual browser settings.

4. GDPR & CCPA Compliance

Because we do not store, harvest, or sell personal identifiers or user photo files, SmartPicTools complies fully with European General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) standards. You possess the right to browse and utilize our services in complete anonymity.

5. Contact Us Regarding Privacy

If you have any questions or require more information regarding our privacy practices, please contact us at support@smartpictools.in.
