Everything you need to know about SmartPicTools privacy, exam requirements, and image conversion.

Are my uploaded photos and documents stored on your servers? ▼

No, never. SmartPicTools is built on a 100% private, client-side architecture using HTML5 Canvas, WebAssembly, and local JavaScript engines. Your pictures, signatures, and confidential PDF files never leave your device and are processed exclusively in your web browser's temporary memory. We do not maintain any storage databases or cloud backups of your files.

How do I compress an image to exact 20 KB or 50 KB for online government exam forms? ▼

Open our dedicated 'Compress Image to Exact KB' tool, select your image, and choose the 20 KB or 50 KB exam preset button. Our iterative compression engine calculates the optimal compression matrix in real time, delivering a file that precisely meets official application portals requirements (like SSC, UPSC, State Police, Railway, and IBPS) while keeping text and facial features clear.

What is the official passport photo size for Indian competitive exams and Visas? ▼

In India, government exams like SSC, UPSC, and State PSCs officially require a 3.5 cm × 4.5 cm photograph with an aspect ratio of 3.5:4.5 and a clean white studio background. For US Visas and international travel, a 2 × 2 inch (51 mm × 51 mm) square format is required. Our Passport Size Photo Maker includes built-in 300 DPI templates for both standards with optional studio cutting lines.

Why do converted vector SVG files remain sharp with 0% blur when zoomed in? ▼

Unlike ordinary online converters that simply embed low-resolution raster bitmaps inside SVG tags, our native vector conversion engine extracts true mathematical curve paths and vector glyphs directly from original documents. When you open the exported SVG in CorelDraw, Adobe Illustrator, or web browsers, every letter and curve remains infinitely scalable without pixelation or quality loss.
