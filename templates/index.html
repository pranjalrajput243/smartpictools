Frequently Asked Questions

Why were there white borders before, and how is it fixed now? ▼

Previously, photos were placed inside fixed vertical A4 dimensions. With Edge-to-Edge mode, our engine matches the PDF page dimensions to the exact width and height of each photo, eliminating white bars 100%.

Can I combine different image formats (JPG, PNG, HEIC) into one PDF? ▼

Yes! You can mix JPG, PNG, WEBP, and Apple iPhone HEIC pictures together into a clean, uniform PDF document.
