Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about SmartPicTools features and security.

Is SmartPicTools really 100% free with unlimited usage? ▼

Yes! SmartPicTools is completely free to use for both personal and commercial projects. There are no hidden subscription fees, watermark restrictions, or daily upload caps.

How does exact KB/MB target compression work? ▼

Unlike traditional tools that only use percentages, our binary search algorithm dynamically tests file sizes in memory to match your exact requested KB or MB value while maintaining maximum visual sharpness.

Are my uploaded images safe and private? ▼

100%. We practice strict in-memory execution. Files are processed directly in temporary RAM buffers and permanently wiped immediately after download. We never store, index, or share your photos.
