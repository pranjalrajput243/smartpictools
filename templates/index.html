Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about SmartPicTools features and security.

1. How does Exact KB/MB target compression work without losing sharpness? ▼

Unlike basic compressors that force a random quality percentage, our engine runs a precision binary search algorithm in browser memory. It calculates the exact byte ratio to match your requested file weight (e.g. 20KB, 50KB) while preserving maximum visual sharpness and clarity.

2. Which aspect ratio templates are supported for social media platforms? ▼

SmartPicTools provides 1-tap presets for Instagram Feed (1:1 Square), YouTube Thumbnails (16:9), Instagram Reels & YouTube Shorts (9:16), and Portrait Posts (4:5), as well as a Freeform drag mode to crop custom dimensions smoothly.

3. How does the Proportion Lock prevent images from stretching or distortion? ▼

When 'Lock Proportions' is checked, changing the Width automatically recalculates the Height proportionally (and vice versa). This ensures your photo maintains its exact natural aspect ratio without getting squished or stretched.

4. Can I convert Apple iPhone (.HEIC / .HEIF) photos into standard JPG or PNG? ▼

Yes! Apple photos saved in HEIC format cannot be viewed on many Android devices or Windows PCs. Our built-in engine automatically decodes HEIC files and converts them directly to universal JPG, PNG, WEBP, or PDF with zero quality loss.

5. How does the Diagonal Grid Watermark protect photos from copyright theft? ▼

While single corner watermarks can easily be cropped out, our Diagonal Grid style tiles your copyright watermark evenly across the entire surface at a subtle, professional transparency (25%), making unauthorized removal impossible.

6. How many files can I convert at once, and how does the Batch ZIP download work? ▼

You can select up to 20 photos simultaneously. When multiple files are processed, SmartPicTools bundles all converted images into a single compressed `.zip` archive for convenient 1-click download.

7. Is SmartPicTools really 100% free and are my uploaded pictures safe and private? ▼

SmartPicTools is 100% free with no hidden fees or subscriptions. Your images never get uploaded to or stored on any external database. All compression and editing executes client-side inside your browser RAM and is wiped immediately upon download.
