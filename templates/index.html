Frequently Asked Questions

Is SmartPicTools really 100% free with unlimited usage? ▼

Yes! SmartPicTools is completely free to use for both personal and commercial projects. There are no subscriptions, watermark restrictions, or daily upload caps.

Are my uploaded images safe and private? ▼

100%. We practice strict in-memory execution. Files are processed directly inside your browser RAM and are never uploaded, stored, or indexed on any remote server.

How does exact KB target compression work? ▼

Our precision binary search algorithm dynamically calculates compression in memory to match your exact requested KB value (e.g. 20KB, 50KB) while maintaining maximum visual sharpness.
