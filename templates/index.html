Frequently Asked Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about SmartPicTools privacy, exam compliance, and file processing.

How does SmartPicTools guarantee 100% private, client-side processing? ▼

Most online file tools upload your private pictures and sensitive PDF documents to remote cloud servers to process them. In contrast, SmartPicTools runs directly inside your web browser using HTML5 Canvas, WebAssembly, and local JavaScript engines. Your documents, signatures, and personal photos never leave your device and are never saved to any database.

Will my compressed photos and joined signatures be accepted by SSC, UPSC, and Police exam portals? ▼

Yes, absolutely. Our 'Compress Image to Exact KB', 'Passport Size Photo Maker', and 'Photo & Signature Joiner' conform strictly to official notification guidelines (such as SSC, UPSC, State Police, Railway, and IBPS). The output images follow exact official dimensions (350×450px or 350×500px), 80/20 proportions, and file weight caps (under 50KB or 20KB).

Does cropping a PDF document break text searchability (Ctrl+F) or blur barcodes? ▼

No. Our 'Crop PDF Online' tool uses a native PDF manipulation engine that adjusts page bounding boxes mathematically rather than rasterizing pages into blurry images. All original vector fonts, selectable text (Ctrl+F works), links, and high-density barcodes remain 100% sharp and readable by laser scanners and PDF readers.

Can I open converted SVG vector files in CorelDraw and Adobe Illustrator without missing fonts? ▼

Yes. When converting PDF or image documents to SVG through our vector engine, typography is converted directly into mathematical vector curves (equivalent to CorelDraw's 'Convert to Curves' Ctrl+Q feature). All objects open unlocked and immediately editable in CorelDraw, Figma, and Illustrator with 0% blur at any zoom scale.

Is SmartPicTools completely free to use for commercial and freelance work? ▼

Yes. All 18 tools and 70+ format conversion hubs on SmartPicTools are 100% free forever. There are no subscriptions, no daily processing limits, no forced registrations, and no watermarks placed on your exported files.

How do touching cutting lines save paper when printing passport photos on A4 or 4x6 sheets? ▼

In traditional photo printing, wide white gaps between individual passport photos force you to make multiple scissor cuts, wasting paper. Our Passport Photo Maker arranges photos with zero-gap touching black cutting lines aligned to the top-left of the sheet. You only need to make single, straight cuts along the shared line, and any unused paper remains clean and reusable.

How does the Suggestion Box work if I want to request a new tool or report an issue? ▼

Clicking the '💡 Suggestions' button in the top navigation opens an instant modal popup right on your screen. You can type your tool request or feedback directly, and our internal system logs your message immediately so our development team can review it.
