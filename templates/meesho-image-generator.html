Can I use these images on Flipkart, Amazon, and Shopsy as well? ▼

Yes. Flipkart, Shopsy, and Amazon all require 1:1 square product listings (preferably 1000×1000 px or larger with clean backgrounds). The compact framing and studio variants work across all major Indian marketplaces.

How do I find out which image gives the lowest shipping fee on Meesho? ▼

Download the full ZIP of 12 variants. On your Meesho Supplier Panel, create a new catalog and upload Variant 1. Note the estimated shipping price. Then test with Variant 2 or Variant 4. Once you see the lowest shipping slab (typically ₹35 - ₹55), finalize that image as your primary cover photo.

How is volumetric weight calculated for courier shipping? ▼

Couriers calculate volumetric weight using the formula: (Length × Breadth × Height in cm) ÷ 5000. Couriers charge based on whichever is higher: the actual dead weight or the volumetric weight. Packaging in compact boxes or polybags ensures you remain in the lowest <500g shipping slab.
