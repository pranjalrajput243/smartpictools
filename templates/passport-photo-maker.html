Frequently Asked Questions

How many passport photos fit on one A4 paper sheet? ▼

A standard A4 sheet can hold up to 30 passport photos (5 columns × 6 rows) at official 3.5 × 4.5 cm dimensions with neat cutting margins.

Can I print just 4 or 6 photos on an A4 sheet? ▼

Yes! You can enter any custom number (like 4, 6, 8, 12). The tool prints only that exact number of photos at the top of the A4 page, leaving the rest of the sheet blank so you can reuse the photo paper later.
