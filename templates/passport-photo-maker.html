Frequently Asked Questions

What is the standard passport photo size for Indian government exam forms? ▼

The official standard for SSC, UPSC, Railway, Police, and bank exams is 3.5 cm width × 4.5 cm height (approx 413 × 531 pixels at 300 DPI) with file size between 20 KB and 50 KB.

How much does it cost to print the 8-photo sheet at a local studio? ▼

Taking the 8-photo sheet on a pen drive or WhatsApp to any photo lab to print on standard 4×6 inch paper typically costs only ₹5 to ₹10, saving you over ₹50 compared to studio shooting charges.
