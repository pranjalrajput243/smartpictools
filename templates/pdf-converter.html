Frequently Asked Questions

What does 300 DPI resolution mean when converting PDF to image? ▼

300 DPI (Dots Per Inch) is the gold standard for high-definition publishing and printing. When converting PDF text and vector graphics to images, 300 DPI ensures text edges stay crisp and readable even when zoomed in, with zero pixelation.

Are my uploaded PDF documents private and secure? ▼

100%. We use Mozilla's client-side PDF.js engine. The PDF renders directly inside your device's browser memory and is never uploaded to any remote server.
