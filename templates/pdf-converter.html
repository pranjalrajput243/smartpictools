Frequently Asked Questions

Does the converted SVG support Ctrl+F searching? ▼

Yes! Our engine exports genuine vector SVG with embedded fonts and selectable text elements. When you open the SVG in Chrome, Edge, or Illustrator, you can press Ctrl+F to search for any text or select words with your mouse.

What does 300 DPI resolution mean for JPG/PNG? ▼

300 DPI (Dots Per Inch) is the gold standard for high-definition publishing and printing. When converting PDF to raster images, 300 DPI ensures text edges stay crisp and readable even when zoomed in.
