Frequently Asked Questions

What is a Favicon and why does every website need one? ▼

A favicon is the small icon that appears next to your website title in browser tabs, bookmarks, and search history. Having a custom favicon increases brand trust and website recognition.

Which size should I use for WordPress and modern websites? ▼

32×32 px (.ico or .png) is the official recommended standard for all modern desktop browsers, WordPress, Shopify, and HTML websites.
