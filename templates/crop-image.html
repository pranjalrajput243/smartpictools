Frequently Asked Questions

How does edge-to-edge aspect ratio coverage work? ▼

When you choose any ratio preset (like 1:1, 16:9, or 9:16), our algorithm calculates the maximum available dimension across the photo. It expands the crop box to cover 100% of the photo from top to bottom (or left to right), avoiding awkward tiny centered boxes.

Why does Zoom Out stop at 100% original size? ▼

Unlike basic tools where zooming out shrinks the photo into an unusable tiny rectangle, our boundary lock clamps the zoom. You can zoom in as much as you like, but zooming out stops precisely at the original photo scale so your photo never detaches or breaks its frame.

How do I pan the photo using the Spacebar Hand tool? ▼

Just like in Adobe Photoshop, hold down the Spacebar key on your keyboard to instantly turn your cursor into a hand. Click and drag the photo anywhere to frame your shot, then release Spacebar to continue resizing the crop box.

Does cropping reduce the resolution or clarity of my picture? ▼

Zero quality loss. The crop calculations execute directly against the original high-resolution pixel matrix of your photo in temporary browser memory, downloading at maximum sharpness.
