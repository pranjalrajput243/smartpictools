Frequently Asked Questions

Can I permanently remove the password from an e-Aadhaar or bank statement PDF? ▼

Yes. Switch to 'Remove Password', upload your document, and enter the password once. The tool decrypts the file and downloads a permanent, password-free PDF that you can open anytime without credentials.

How strong is the password protection added by this tool? ▼

We use industry-standard AES-256 bit encryption (the same standard used by financial institutions and government agencies). Without the exact password, the document content cannot be read or viewed by any PDF software.

Can this tool unlock a PDF if I completely forgot the password? ▼

No. For legal and cybersecurity reasons, you must enter the correct password to decrypt open-protected files. Modern AES-256 encryption is mathematically unbreakable without the authorized password.

Does locking or unlocking a PDF degrade its text clarity or resolution? ▼

No. The document's internal text objects, font streams, hyperlinks, and vector sharpness remain 100% untouched. Only the encryption security wrapper is added or removed.

Are my passwords or confidential documents saved or stored anywhere? ▼

Never. Passwords and documents are processed strictly in temporary execution memory and immediately wiped. SmartPicTools never stores, logs, or tracks your files or passwords.
