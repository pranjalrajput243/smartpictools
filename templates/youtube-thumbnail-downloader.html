Frequently Asked Questions

Why does YouTube add blurred bars on the sides of Shorts thumbnails? ▼

YouTube's image CDN defaults to a 16:9 landscape aspect ratio. When a 9:16 vertical Shorts video is uploaded, YouTube pads the left and right sides with blurred borders. Our tool detects this and extracts only the pure 9:16 vertical crop.

Can I download in 4K resolution? ▼

Yes. By selecting the 'Download 4K' option, our backend applies high-precision Lanczos interpolation to upscale the master image to 3840×2160 pixels with sharp edges.

Is it legal to download YouTube video thumbnails? ▼

Yes. Saving thumbnails for personal use, design inspiration, or educational presentations falls under fair use. However, re-uploading someone else's thumbnail on your own video without permission is a copyright violation.
