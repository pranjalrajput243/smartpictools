Frequently Asked Questions

Can I combine photos with different sizes and orientations into a single PDF? ▼

Yes. Our compiler evaluates each image individually. A landscape photo will generate a landscape PDF page, and a portrait photo will generate a portrait PDF page, keeping all images centered with zero stretching or distortion.

Why does SmartPicTools produce 0% white borders around pictures? ▼

Traditional tools constrain photos onto fixed standard paper sheets (like A4), creating empty white borders on the sides. Our tool scales the PDF page viewport to match the exact image boundaries, producing a clean, edge-to-edge document.

Can I convert Apple iPhone HEIC pictures directly to PDF? ▼

Yes. Modern iOS photos (.heic/.heif) are decoded client-side inside your browser, allowing you to select them alongside standard JPGs or PNGs and combine them seamlessly into one PDF file.

Is there a daily limit on how many images I can convert to PDF? ▼

No limits. SmartPicTools is 100% free with unlimited conversions. You can merge as many pages and create as many PDF documents as you need without subscriptions, registrations, or watermarks.

Are my private invoices and personal ID proofs saved on your servers? ▼

Never. SmartPicTools operates on a strict zero-upload architecture. Everything is compiled directly inside your browser’s temporary memory, and all data is permanently erased the moment you close the tab.
