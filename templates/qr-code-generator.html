Frequently Asked Questions

Will the QR codes generated on SmartPicTools expire or stop working? ▼

No. All QR codes and barcodes created on SmartPicTools are static. Your destination URL or text is encoded directly into the pixel pattern. Because there is no intermediary redirect server or subscription involved, the codes will work permanently for the lifetime of your printed material.

Can I create a UPI QR code for Google Pay, PhonePe, and Paytm payments? ▼

Yes. You can enter a standard NPCI UPI link format (e.g. upi://pay?pa=yourvpa@bank&pn=YourName). When customers scan the printed QR code with any UPI app (GPay, PhonePe, Paytm, or BHIM), it opens directly to payment.

What type of barcode is generated, and is it compatible with inventory scanners? ▼

Our barcode engine generates high-density Code 128 barcodes. Code 128 is an international standard that supports both alphanumeric text and numbers, making it 100% compatible with retail POS systems, warehouse inventory scanners, and logistics tracking software.

Is there any limit on how many QR codes or barcodes I can create? ▼

No limits. You can create and download unlimited high-resolution codes completely free for personal or commercial projects without account sign-ups, fees, or watermarks.

Are my Wi-Fi credentials or URLs saved on your servers? ▼

Never. The entire encoding process executes locally inside your web browser using client-side JavaScript. Your Wi-Fi network passwords, payment handles, and private text never leave your device.
