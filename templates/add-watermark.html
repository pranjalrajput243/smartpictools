Frequently Asked Questions

Can someone easily remove a diagonal grid watermark with AI or basic photo tools? ▼

No. While a single corner logo can easily be cropped or covered, our repeated diagonal grid crosses over complex facial features, lighting gradients, and fine textures. Removing it with AI leaves obvious blurring and destroys the image quality, making theft pointless.

What is the recommended opacity percentage for photography client proofs? ▼

For professional client proofs (wedding samples, portrait shoots, or architecture proofs), an opacity between 20% and 30% is ideal. It allows clients to clearly evaluate facial expressions and composition while making the proofs unusable for printing without final payment.

Can I add both custom text watermarks and transparent PNG company logos? ▼

Yes. You can enter any custom copyright text (e.g., © 2026 YourBrand Name, 'SAMPLE', or 'CONFIDENTIAL') with custom font sizes and colors, or upload a transparent PNG logo stamp to position directly on your pictures.

Does watermarking reduce the original resolution or clarity of my picture? ▼

No. Our engine renders the watermark vector layers directly on top of your photo’s native resolution canvas. Original megapixels, sharpness, and color profiles remain 100% intact.

Are my unpublished copyrighted photos saved on your servers? ▼

Never. SmartPicTools is built on a 100% private in-browser architecture. Your photos never leave your computer or smartphone, and all temporary canvas memory is erased when you close your browser tab.
