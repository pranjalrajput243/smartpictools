Yes. You can either click on those exact pages in the visual grid or type comma-separated numbers like 1, 5, 12 in the range box. The tool will compile only those specific pages into your new PDF.

Standard web tools convert pages into pictures before recombining them, destroying text data. SmartPicTools extracts native PDF page structures directly, keeping character streams, search capabilities, and hyperlinks intact.
